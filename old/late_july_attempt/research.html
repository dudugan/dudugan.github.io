Research

I'm interested in animals (including humans), aliens, artificial intelligence, and ancient people. More specifically, I'm interested in developing accurate formalisms for how these groups might communicate, and aligning them with realistic and precise hypotheses about evolution and emergence. I'm also hoping this understanding could have practical benefits, e.g. creating doolittle devices and mitigating catastrophic risks from misaligned AI.

I think misaligned AI is the greatest extinction threat humanity has faced. We should probably do something to stop it. To that end I work on understanding LLM communication, both between LLMs and between different instances of the same LLM. I attempt to find protocols which minimize the possibility that capable models will become misaligned or have the opportunity to act on this misalignment.
