# Affiliations

This summer, I volunteered for a month at [the Tbilisi Zoo](http://www.zoo.ge/index.php?lng=eng) as an assistant to the head Head Zookeeper of Carnivores & Primates. I also conducted fieldwork on Georgian morphosyntax for a month with [Richard Luo](https://ling.yale.edu/profile/richard-luo) and [Natasha Thalluri](https://sites.harvard.edu/natasha-thalluri/). In August, I'm preparing for [BlueDot Impact](https://bluedot.org/courses/technical-ai-safety)'s Technical intensive AI Safety program. In the coming year, I'm looking forward to continuing my work composing for [Amoriem Labs](https://amoriem-labs.github.io), Yale's game development organization, and possibly applying to work with a [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) AI safety project. 

Recently, I retired as editor-in-chief of [the Cortex Collective](https://www.cortexmagazine.com), an experimental multimedia scifi arts collective at Yale. I also worked part-time at [the Center for Collaborative Arts & Media](https://ccam.yale.edu/people/david-garsten) for two years, and did research with [the Yale Grammatical Diversity Project](https://ygdp.yale.edu/). I'm a member of the [Yale Syntax Reading Group](https://yalelingevent.github.io/synrg/). 

In past lives, I cofounded [LingLeague](https://linguisticsleague.org) and worked as editor-in-chief of [Choate Rosemary Hall](https://www.choate.edu)'s oldest literary magazine. I'm also a [Franke](https://frankeprogram.yale.edu/) Fellow, a [Bronfman](https://bronfman.org/) Fellow, a [YAIPI](https://www.yaleaipolicy.org/) Turing Fellow, a [YEA](https://www.yaleeffectivealtruism.com/) Symposium Fellow, and an [Atlas](https://www.atlasfellowship.org/) Awardee.
